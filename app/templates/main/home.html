Everything a small maintenance team actually needs

No training required for the people on the floor. They point a phone camera at a sticker.

Scan-to-report

Every machine carries a QR label. Scanning it opens a form that already knows which machine it is, so nobody types an asset tag wrong or reports the wrong lathe.

Severity that means something

Low, medium or high — chosen by the person standing at the machine. The queue sorts itself, so the press that stopped the line outranks the flickering work light.

One queue per company

Technicians claim a request, move it to In Progress, and close it as Repair Complete or mark the machine Down. Nothing gets lost in a group chat.

Permanent service history

A repair cannot be closed without a maintenance log: what was done, which parts went in, how many hours. Two years later you can still see why that spindle keeps failing.

Roles, not guesswork

Crew members report. Technicians repair. Administrators manage accounts. Each person sees exactly the tabs their job needs and nothing else.

Your data stays yours

Every company is isolated from every other company on the platform. A request, a machine or an employee is only ever visible to the company that owns it.

How it works

Four steps, from a fault on the floor to a closed repair with a paper trail.

STEP 01

Register your company

The owner creates the company account and becomes its administrator, then adds crew members and technicians.

STEP 02

Label your machines

Add each machine to the registry and print its QR label. Stick it somewhere the operator can reach.

STEP 03

Crew reports a fault

Scan, choose a severity, describe the problem. The request lands in the queue instantly.

STEP 04

Technician closes it out

Claim it, fix it, file the maintenance log. The machine goes back to operational with its history updated.
